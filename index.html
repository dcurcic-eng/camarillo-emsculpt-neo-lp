Questions

Before you book,the honest answers.

Does it hurt?

It feels like an intense but tolerable workout with warmth. Intensity is raised gradually and lowered whenever you ask. Soreness like after hard exercise settles in 24 to 48 hours.

How many sessions will I need?

A typical course is 4 sessions spaced 5 to 10 days apart. With this offer, your 4th session is free. Dr. Dooman confirms the right plan at your assessment.

When will I see changes?

First signs usually appear 2 to 4 weeks in, with full effect 8 to 12 weeks after your last session. Many patients first notice better endurance sitting or standing upright.

Is it better than exercise?

It is not a replacement for movement. Emsculpt NEO drives deeper, stronger contractions than you can do on your own, which helps when floor work or core training is hard on your back. Most patients pair it with simple home exercise.

Is Emsculpt NEO for men too?

Yes. Men and women both use it to rebuild abdominal and gluteal strength. The range-of-motion results above include several male patients.

Who should not have it?

It is not for anyone with metal or electronic implants near the treatment area, a pacemaker or neurostimulator, pregnancy, active malignancy, fever, or recent surgery in the area until cleared.

Does it treat back pain?

Emsculpt NEO is a muscle conditioning device, not a treatment for spinal pathology. It strengthens the trunk muscles that support your spine, alongside your chiropractic care.

Can I combine it with adjustments, decompression or Emsella?

Yes. Sessions pair naturally with adjustments, decompression and laser therapy. Dr. Dooman plans the order and spacing around what your exam shows.

Does it burn fat?

Fat reduction is a documented secondary effect of the RF component. At CSNI the focus is muscle that supports your spine, so any contouring is a bonus.

How much does it cost, and is financing available?

Pricing is quoted at your consultation. Book the 4-session course and your 4th session is free. We accept major cards and offer financing through CareCredit and WellFit.
